How to Name Your Startup

Choosing the right name for your startup is one of the most impactful decisions you'll make. A great name is short, memorable, easy to spell, and ideally hints at what your business does — all while being unique enough to build a brand around.

Our Startup Name Generator uses smart naming patterns — compound words, prefixes, suffixes, portmanteaus, and industry-specific blends — to give you a diverse list of candidates. From there, check domain availability, social media handles, and trademark databases before committing.

🧩

Compound Names

Blend two meaningful words together — like Facebook, YouTube, or Snapchat. Simple, powerful, easy to remember.

⚡

Prefix & Suffix

Add modern tech prefixes (Neo, Meta, Hyper) or trendy suffixes (Ly, ify, io) to any keyword for an instant brand feel.

🔀

Portmanteau

Merge the start of one word with the end of another to create a totally unique, made-up brand name that's still intuitive.

Tips for Picking the Perfect Name

  • ✓ Keep it under 3 syllables — shorter names stick better in memory.
  • ✓ Make sure it's easy to spell when heard aloud — avoid confusing letter combos.
  • ✓ Check that the .com domain is available before falling in love with a name.
  • ✓ Search USPTO or your country's trademark database to avoid legal issues.
  • ✓ Test it with 5–10 people from your target market — first impressions matter.
  • ✓ Think about how it will look as a logo, domain, and social handle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the best name for my startup?
A great startup name should be short, memorable, easy to spell, and reflect your brand's core value. It should also have an available domain name. Use our generator to explore creative combinations based on your keywords and industry, then shortlist 3–5 candidates and test them with your target audience before making a final decision.
What makes a startup name memorable and effective?
Memorable startup names tend to be 1–3 syllables, easy to pronounce and spell, unique enough to stand out, and ideally evoke the product's purpose or emotion. Patterns like compound words (e.g., Facebook), prefix/suffix combos (e.g., Dropbox), or playful modifications (e.g., Lyft, Fiverr) have proven successful. Avoid names that are too generic, too long, or already trademarked.
Should my startup name include my industry keywords?
Including industry keywords can help customers immediately understand what your startup does, which is great for SEO and first impressions. However, too literal a name can limit your brand as you grow. A balanced approach is to use an industry-related word as a prefix or suffix while pairing it with a unique keyword — for example, "HealthWave" or "EcoForge". Our generator provides both keyword-heavy and more abstract name options.

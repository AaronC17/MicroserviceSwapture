About Temporary Email

What is Temp Mail?

A temporary (disposable) email address lets you receive emails without revealing your real email. Great for signing up for services you're not sure about, avoiding spam, or testing email functionality during development.

How it works

  • A random address is generated automatically
  • Inbox refreshes every 30 seconds
  • Click any message to read it
  • Generate a new address any time

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the email address last?

The address is session-based. It will work as long as the page is open. Generating a new address creates a completely fresh inbox.

Can I send emails from this address?

No. This service is receive-only. You can only read emails sent to your temporary address.

Is this secure?

Temp mail is great for protecting your real email from spam, but don't use it for sensitive accounts. Emails are stored on 1secmail.com servers and are publicly accessible if someone knows your address.

Why am I not receiving emails?

Some services block known disposable email domains. Try using a different service or check that you copied the address correctly. The inbox auto-refreshes every 30 seconds, or click Refresh manually.

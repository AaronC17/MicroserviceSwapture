About QR Code Generator

What is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional barcode that can store various types of information. Smartphones can scan them instantly using the built-in camera app to open URLs, connect to WiFi, call phone numbers, and more.

Supported QR Types

  • URL: Link to any website
  • Text: Plain text message
  • Email: Open an email compose window
  • Phone: Dial a phone number
  • SMS: Send a text message
  • WiFi: Connect to a wireless network

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these QR codes free to use?

Yes, completely free. QR codes are generated using the qrserver.com API and can be used for any personal or commercial purpose.

How do I scan a QR code?

Open your smartphone camera app and point it at the QR code. On most modern phones, a notification will pop up letting you open the link or action directly.

What size should I use?

For most uses, 300px is fine for digital display. For printing, use 500px or larger to ensure the code remains scannable after printing.

Can I use a custom color?

Yes! Choose any foreground and background color. Make sure there is sufficient contrast between them — low contrast QR codes may be difficult to scan.

About the SwapTure Unit Converter

The SwapTure Unit Converter is a fast, accurate, and free tool for converting between the most common measurement units used in everyday life, science, and engineering. Whether you need to convert kilometers to miles, kilograms to pounds, Celsius to Fahrenheit, or liters to gallons, SwapTure gives you instant results as you type.

Our converter supports five major categories: Length (meters, feet, inches, miles, nautical miles, and more), Weight (kilograms, pounds, ounces, stones, and more), Temperature (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin), Volume (liters, gallons, cups, fluid ounces, and more), and Speed (km/h, mph, knots, m/s, ft/s).

All conversions use precise internationally accepted conversion factors. The result updates in real time — no need to press a button. You can also swap the direction of conversion with a single click, and view a full conversion table for any unit category.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?
To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply the Celsius value by 9/5 and add 32. The formula is: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. For example, 100°C equals 212°F. Use the Temperature tab above for instant conversions.
How many feet are in a meter?
There are exactly 3.28084 feet in one meter. Conversely, one foot equals 0.3048 meters exactly (by international definition). Use the Length tab above to convert between meters, feet, inches, miles, and more.
How many kilograms are in a pound?
One pound (lb) equals 0.453592 kilograms. Therefore, to convert pounds to kilograms, multiply by 0.453592. To convert kilograms to pounds, multiply by 2.20462. Use the Weight tab above for fast, accurate conversions.

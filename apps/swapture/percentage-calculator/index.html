Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate what percent of a number something is? ▾

To find X% of Y, multiply Y by X and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 = (20 ÷ 100) × 150 = 30. Use Card 1 above to do this instantly.

How do I find what percentage one number is of another? ▾

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 30 is what percent of 150? (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%. Card 2 handles this for you automatically.

How do I calculate percentage change between two numbers? ▾

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the absolute value of the original, then multiply by 100. From 80 to 100: ((100 − 80) ÷ 80) × 100 = 25% increase. Card 3 shows this with a color indicator.

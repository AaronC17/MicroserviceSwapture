About This Word Counter Tool

Whether you're a student checking an essay's length, a blogger optimizing a post, or a professional meeting tight copy requirements, our free word counter gives you instant, accurate statistics. Simply type or paste your content into the text area above and watch all six metrics update in real time — no button press needed.

What does each statistic mean?

  • Words — Total number of whitespace-separated tokens in your text.
  • Characters (with spaces) — Every character including spaces, tabs, and line breaks.
  • Characters (without spaces) — Only non-whitespace characters; useful for SMS or tweet limits.
  • Sentences — Approximate sentence count based on terminal punctuation (. ! ?).
  • Paragraphs — Non-empty blocks of text separated by blank lines.
  • Reading time — Estimated at 200 words per minute, rounded up to the nearest minute.

SwapTure's word counter works entirely in your browser — your text is never sent to any server, ensuring full privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter work?

Our word counter splits your text by whitespace and counts each non-empty token as a word. It updates in real time as you type or paste text into the editor, so you always have an accurate count instantly.

What is the difference between characters with and without spaces?

Characters with spaces counts every single character in your text, including spaces, tabs, and newlines. Characters without spaces counts only non-whitespace characters, which is useful for platforms that restrict visible character counts.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute. The total word count is divided by 200 and rounded up to the nearest minute. Texts under one minute display as "< 1 min read".
